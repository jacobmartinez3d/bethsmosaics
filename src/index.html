My Process

Mosaics are intricate works that bring color and personality to spaces. My creations are made with found and re-purposed materials, giving new life to previously loved items. My art is inspired by the beauty of Colorado, the Southwest, and Mexico.

  1. Step 1:

    Finding furniture , frames, and tiles to re-purpose.

  2. Step 2:

    Preparing a design. Cutting and gluing tiles.

  3. Step 3:

    Grouting
